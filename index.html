Our Story • Founded by Mehak

A Symphony of Spices,
Rooted in Royal Tradition

Nestled near the historic India Gate in New Delhi, The Urban Plate was founded by Mehak with a singular passion: to elevate authentic Indian culinary heritage into an unforgettable fine dining encounter. Each recipe honors generational cooking traditions — from hand-ground masalas and coastal infusions to slow-cooked tandoori delicacies.

Under Mehak's culinary vision, our kitchen celebrates the bounty of Indian agriculture, sourcing directly from organic spice estates in Kerala, saffron orchards of Kashmir, and local farmsteads. Guided by the revered Indian philosophy of Atithi Devo Bhava ("the guest is divine"), we invite you to experience hospitality that touches the soul.
