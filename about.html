Our Story

A passion for food, a commitment to excellence

Who We Are

From Humble Beginnings
to an Award-Winning Table

The Urban Plate was founded in 2015 by Chef Marcus Ellwood, a Cornish-born cook whose two decades in Michelin-starred kitchens across London, Paris, and Tokyo had given him an insatiable hunger for something more personal. "I wanted a space where every guest could feel the story behind every plate," Marcus recalls. "Not theatre — just honesty on a fork."

What began as a 30-cover neighbourhood bistro nestled near India Gate on Rajpath quickly earned a devoted following. Within two years the team expanded, the kitchen was rebuilt, and The Urban Plate cemented its reputation as one of New Delhi's most exciting contemporary dining destinations — recognised by premier food guides and awarded Best Fine Dining Restaurant at the Hospitality Awards.

At the heart of everything we do is a philosophy of celebrating local, seasonal British ingredients through the lens of global technique. We collaborate directly with farmers in Kent, Devon, and Yorkshire, visiting fields and changing our menu as the seasons turn. The result is food that tastes exactly like it should: vivid, honest, and rooted in place.

What Drives Us

Our Core Values

Three principles guide every decision we make — from the suppliers we choose to the welcome we give.

🌱

Farm to Fork

We work directly with over twenty local farmers and artisan producers to bring the finest seasonal ingredients to your table. Traceability isn't a buzzword here — it's a promise we keep with every dish we serve.

🍽️

Craft & Creativity

Every dish is an expression of our kitchen's curiosity and skill. We draw on global culinary traditions to create food that is both surprising and deeply comforting — refined without being fussy, creative without being chaotic.

🤝

Community First

We give back to our local community through our monthly supper clubs for neighbourhood charities, our apprenticeship programme for young chefs, and our commitment to reducing food waste to near zero through our daily staff meals and local food banks.

The Faces Behind Every Plate

The People Behind the Plate

Our team brings together decades of international experience with a shared love of great food and genuine hospitality.

Chef Marcus Ellwood

Head Chef & Founder

With 20 years of experience across Michelin-starred kitchens in London, Paris, and Tokyo, Marcus founded The Urban Plate to bring world-class cooking to a genuinely welcoming neighbourhood table. His menus change with the seasons and always tell a story.

Sophie Laurent

Sous Chef

Sophie brings her Parisian training from Le Cordon Bleu and three years at a two-star Lyonnaise bouchon to our kitchen. Her mastery of classical French technique, combined with a love of bold British flavours, makes her an indispensable creative partner.

James Okafor

Pastry Chef

James's desserts have been described as "edible poetry" by the Evening Standard. Drawing on his Nigerian heritage and his time at The Fat Duck, he crafts confections that are equal parts nostalgia and surprise — the perfect final chapter to every meal.

Priya Sharma

Restaurant Manager

Priya ensures every guest feels at home from the moment they walk through the door. With a background in luxury hotel management and an infectious warmth, she sets the tone for a dining experience that is as effortless as it is memorable.
