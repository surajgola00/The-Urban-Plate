Our Vision & Heritage

From A Passionate Dream
to an Award-Winning Table

The Urban Plate was founded by culinary visionary and restaurateur Mehak, driven by a deep love for India's rich gastronomic traditions and contemporary dining artistry. Having explored diverse culinary capitals across the globe, Mehak returned with an ambitious dream: to build a dining sanctuary where the warmth of Indian hospitality meets modern culinary excellence. "I wanted a space where every guest feels the soul behind every spice and every plate," Mehak reflects. "Atithi Devo Bhava — welcoming our guests with genuine warmth and uncompromised flavour."

What began as a boutique dining experience nestled near the historic India Gate on Rajpath quickly earned an ardent following among food lovers, diplomats, and connoisseurs. Guided by Mehak's creative vision, the kitchen was crafted to celebrate seasonal treasures from across India — from fragrant saffron of Kashmir and hand-picked spices of Kerala to organic produce from regional farms — cementing The Urban Plate as one of New Delhi's premier dining destinations.

At the heart of our craft is a culinary philosophy that honors authentic Indian roots while embracing refined global presentation. We collaborate directly with organic growers and artisanal spice purveyors across the subcontinent. The result is food that tastes vivid, heartfelt, and deeply rooted in heritage.

What Drives Us

Our Core Values

Three principles guide every decision we make — from the suppliers we choose to the welcome we give.

🌱

Farm to Fork

We work directly with over twenty local farmers and artisan producers to bring the finest seasonal ingredients to your table. Traceability isn't a buzzword here — it's a promise we keep with every dish we serve.

🍽️

Craft & Creativity

Every dish is an expression of our kitchen's curiosity and skill. We draw on global culinary traditions to create food that is both surprising and deeply comforting — refined without being fussy, creative without being chaotic.

🤝

Community First

We give back to our local community through our monthly supper clubs for neighbourhood charities, our apprenticeship programme for young chefs, and our commitment to reducing food waste to near zero through our daily staff meals and local food banks.

The Faces Behind Every Plate

The People Behind the Plate

Our team brings together decades of international experience with a shared love of great food and genuine hospitality.

Mehak

Founder & Creative Director

The heart and visionary behind The Urban Plate, Mehak conceived the restaurant as an ode to India's timeless culinary heritage paired with modern elegance. Her leadership, warmth, and relentless dedication to authentic tastes define every moment of the guest experience.

Chef Vikram Rathore

Executive Head Chef

Bringing over 18 years of fine dining mastery across India and Europe, Chef Vikram translates Mehak's creative vision into unforgettable culinary journeys, balancing traditional clay-oven mastery with refined modern flair.

Aarav Sen

Head of Pastry & Confections

Aarav reimagines classic Indian sweets and artisanal desserts — from saffron-infused fondants to pistachio kulfi crumbles — crafting sweet finales that are both nostalgic and thrillingly contemporary.

Priya Sharma

General & Hospitality Manager

Priya ensures every guest experiences the heartfelt grace of Indian hospitality from the moment they enter. Her attentiveness and welcoming smile make every dining occasion effortless and memorable.
